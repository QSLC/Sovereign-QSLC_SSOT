Build with QSLC.

This page is the public entry point for QSLC jobs and onboarding. It intentionally does not collect or display Social Security numbers, identity documents, banking details, payroll records, passwords, or authentication codes.

Application checklist

  1. Review the role and public job status.
  2. Email a resume or short work-history summary to QSLC HR.
  3. Complete a private interview/role review.
  4. Receive confirmed title, classification, rate, schedule and start date.
  5. Complete secure I-9, W-4 and payroll onboarding through the approved provider workflow.
  6. Receive QSLC Teams/SharePoint access and timekeeping instructions.
