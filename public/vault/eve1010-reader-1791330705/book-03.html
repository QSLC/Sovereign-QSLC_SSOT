Expanded 2026 Edition

Expanded Chapter 8 — What a Theory Is Allowed to Claim

A theory book becomes more interesting when it states its limits. Conscious Energy Continuum borrows language from physics because physics offers powerful concepts for fields, pressure, entropy, coherence, energy, and information. But a borrowed vocabulary does not turn an operational model into a discovered physical law.

The correct method is layered. Layer one contains established equations or scientific references. Layer two contains project-specific mathematical models. Layer three contains software metrics and business ratios. Layer four contains metaphors and control-language used to make the system easier to reason about. The layers can interact, but they should not be mislabeled.

This distinction is especially important for patent and publishing work. A standard equation cannot become proprietary simply because it appears in a private workbook. What may be protectable is a novel, concrete implementation: a specific data-processing architecture, control loop, verification process, or technical improvement built around those ingredients. This book therefore treats the formulas as lineage, not trophies.

Expanded Chapter 9 — PSI Is Three Different Things

One source of confusion in the project is the word PSI. In different contexts it can mean a conceptual symbol, an internal index, or a token/ledger identifier. Those are not interchangeable. A reader should be able to ask, “Which PSI?” and get a precise answer.

In the corrected master workbook, a PSI token supply of 1,000,001.33 is recorded, along with an internal reference price of $0.034 and an internal reference value of $34,000.04522. This publishing review did not locate a reliable public market source for the referenced contract, so the price is not presented here as a live market quote. That restraint is part of the method.

A useful reader exercise is to track the three PSI layers separately: symbol, ledger quantity, and market observation. If a market source becomes available, record the URL, time, pair, liquidity, price, and whether the quote is reproducible. Never overwrite the internal reference value with a market observation without keeping the old value and its source. The difference between them can itself become a useful metric.
