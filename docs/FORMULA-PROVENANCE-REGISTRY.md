# QSLC / EVE-1010 Formula Provenance Registry

This is the public-safe formula lineage index for the current corrected 18-position registry.

It is intentionally conservative: established physics is labeled as established physics; QSLC-specific models are labeled as models; KPIs are not described as physical laws; conceptual controls are not presented as experimentally validated findings; and F-07 remains incomplete rather than being invented.

The machine-readable source is `public/formula-provenance.json`.

## Governance rules

- Every published formula must retain an ID, name, expression or explicit null, classification, status, and provenance note where needed.
- A known scientific equation does not become a QSLC invention by being included in the Living Calculator.
- A project-specific combination, implementation, transformation, control system, measurement method, or architecture may be separately analyzed for IP protection if supported by human-conception evidence.
- No registry entry may be labeled VERIFIED_MEASUREMENT without source evidence and units.
- No entry may be labeled patent pending without a verified filing receipt/application record.
- F-07 remains INCOMPLETE_DEFINITION until a dated authoritative source supplies its equation.
- Historical registries that used the same F-number differently must be preserved as versioned lineage, not overwritten.
