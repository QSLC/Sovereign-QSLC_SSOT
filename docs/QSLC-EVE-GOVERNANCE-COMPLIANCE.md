# QSLC / EVE Governance & Compliance Baseline

Status: ACTIVE INTERNAL CONTROL BASELINE
Owner: Quantum Sovereign Logistics Corp.
Public system: https://qslc-hei.com
Public contact: qslc1010@qslc-hei.com

## 1. Governance principle

QSLC may create internal policies, product rules, naming conventions, approval controls, records, licenses, documentation, and operating procedures for its own systems. Internal policy does not override federal, state, local, platform, tax, securities, intellectual-property, privacy, employment, consumer-protection, banking, payment-network, or contractual law.

No internal QSLC record may state that a filing, registration, patent, trademark, license, approval, certification, bank connection, payout, publication, customer transaction, or regulatory status exists unless external evidence verifies it.

## 2. Evidence rule

No Evidence = No Approval.

Statuses:
- VERIFIED: supported by a primary source or provider record.
- ACTIVE INTERNAL: created and adopted by QSLC, but not a government or platform approval.
- PENDING EXTERNAL: requires action or approval by a third party.
- REVIEW: evidence conflicts or is incomplete.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence.

## 3. Public advertising rule

Every public claim must be truthful, non-deceptive, and supportable by evidence. Never fabricate users, customers, revenue, testimonials, reviews, partnerships, certifications, market results, or capabilities. Material relationships and paid/incentivized endorsements must be clearly disclosed near the endorsement.

## 4. Digital-asset / PSI rule

PSI is separated from QSLC books, software subscriptions, corporate equity, revenue rights, dividends, profit-sharing, lending, and guaranteed returns unless a legally reviewed and separately documented offering explicitly says otherwise.

Public PSI materials must:
- identify the exact token contract;
- provide independent verification links where available;
- describe protocol mechanics factually;
- avoid promising profit, appreciation, liquidity, bonding-curve completion, exchange listing, yield, revenue rights, dividends, or guaranteed outcomes;
- state that digital assets can lose all value;
- never use QSLC book/software purchases as an investment inducement.

Any token fundraising, sale of investment rights, revenue participation, profit-sharing, equity-like rights, or similar capital-raising structure requires specific securities/tax/legal review before launch.

## 5. Publishing / KDP rule

AI-generated text, images, or translations must be disclosed to KDP when Amazon requires that disclosure. AI-assisted editing is treated according to the platform's current published definitions.

No book may claim:
- Patent Pending unless a filing receipt verifies it.
- Patented unless the issued patent is verified.
- Trademark registration unless registration is verified.
- Guaranteed financial or technological outcomes.
- Ownership of third-party scientific laws, equations, platforms, brands, libraries, or public-domain facts.

## 6. IP classification

Each formula, diagram, workflow, and software module should carry one of:
- Established science/reference
- QSLC project-specific model
- Operational KPI/metric
- Configured constant
- Software implementation
- Trade secret / confidential method
- Pending IP review

Public disclosure must not unintentionally publish information QSLC intends to preserve as a trade secret.

## 7. Commerce

Public prices must identify whether they are monthly, annual, one-time, starting, or custom quote.

Checkout pages must not imply:
- guaranteed delivery before fulfillment capability exists;
- guaranteed income or ROI;
- investment returns;
- regulatory approval.

Refund/access terms, customer support contact, privacy notice, and digital-delivery expectations must be available before purchase.

## 8. Financial routing

Provider records control. Website copy, screenshots, SSOT values, and internal labels do not establish bank linkage or payout success.

A payout destination is VERIFIED only when the payment provider reports it as verified and active/default as intended. Bank identifiers must remain private.

## 9. Privacy/security

Public systems must exclude:
- bank/account numbers
- payroll/check data
- credentials/API keys
- private SSOT values
- owner evidence
- personal addresses unless intentionally published for a legal/business reason
- private device/camera/GPS information
- confidential implementation data

Use least-privilege access and keep admin actions outside the public surface.

## 10. Automation

Automation may draft, validate, publish low-risk pre-approved content, update internal records, and surface alerts where provider permissions allow it.

Automation must stop or escalate when:
- a provider requires OAuth/user consent;
- a legal filing requires certification/signature;
- a payment/payout/account change requires identity or bank verification;
- facts conflict;
- a public claim lacks evidence;
- an action would expose private data;
- platform terms require human acceptance.

## 11. Corporate records

Material governance changes should be logged with:
- date/time
- owner/approver
- policy version
- source/evidence link
- affected system
- status
- next review date

This document is an internal operating control framework, not a substitute for advice from licensed counsel, a tax professional, or a regulated financial professional where law requires or the risk warrants it.
