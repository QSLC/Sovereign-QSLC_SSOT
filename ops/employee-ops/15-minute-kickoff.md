# QSLC Employee Operations — 15-Minute Kickoff

**Purpose:** explain the operating model and capture decisions; this meeting does not itself approve hires, compensation, or payroll changes.

| Time | Topic | Required outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–2:00 | QSLC mission and departments | Confirm business lines and accountable lead |
| 2:00–5:00 | Roles and reporting | Approve role titles, manager, responsibilities, access |
| 5:00–8:00 | Compensation | Record pay type, rate/salary, overtime classification, effective date, approver |
| 8:00–11:00 | Mobile operations | Demonstrate Paychex Flex login, assigned schedule, authorized clock-in/out and correction process |
| 11:00–13:00 | Security and files | Confirm MFA, Teams/SharePoint access, confidentiality and role boundaries |
| 13:00–15:00 | Launch actions | Name owner and due date for each onboarding, payroll and app configuration task |

## Decision register

Record each item as APPROVED, REJECTED, or UNVERIFIED:
- C-corporation status: owner-stated; supporting tax/corporate record still to be checked.
- Paychex Flex plan/features: confirm mobile time punches, geofencing, approvals, and integration/export capability.
- Pay frequency and first payday: proposed biweekly; not configured or approved.
- Each job's employee/contractor classification, pay basis, rate, overtime status, hours, supervisor and start date.
- Remote work eligibility and authorized work locations.
- Required Microsoft 365 licenses and employee identities.
- Employee portal launch scope and access matrix.

## Meeting rules

Do not announce a salary, start date, or payday unless it is approved and funded. Do not share SSNs, tax forms, bank details, health data, or private employee information in the meeting recording or public QSLC repository.
